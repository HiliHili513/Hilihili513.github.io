A Treatise on Trains, Thought, and the Temerity of the Bourgeoisie

I like trains. I really like trains. My thesis is that I love to ride on trains, taking me here and there. Sometimes, I don't pay for my ticket. Sometimes, the ticketmaster asks me to get off the train. Sometimes, I tell the ticketmaster that I can't, since the train is still moving and my mother wouldn't like that I'm doing something dangerous like jumping off a moving train traveling at lower limit velocities of 55mph. Sometimes, the ticketmaster chuckles and grins like the Grinch, pulling the emergency stop. Sometimes, I am forced to walk a lonely road, the only road that I have ever known. Sometimes, I don't know where I might end up….

So my favorite kind of train is a train of thought. Like, you ever wonder how your thoughts can carry you across multiple different areas of life, inevitably ending back up right where you started, dozing off in Circuits class, back to reality — oh there goes gravity.

OK…Gravity makes no sense. Like what does the earth have against me leaving? Is the earth a toxic mother never willing to let her children go beyond the nest, only to end up right back where we started, sitting at the dinner table, drinking a glass of red, red wine…its up to yoooouu! My grade is up to you, only you can decide its fate now…

You have never seen a paper like this before, have you? Like isn't it scary that a student has the omnipotent power to make you read literally anything they submit, and you still have to give them a grade based on their work performance or lack thereof?
